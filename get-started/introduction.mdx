Parahelp is the platform for building your AI support agent. Your agent uses all your tools to resolve your most complex tickets end to end.

The **Customer Agent** resolves support tickets end to end through your customer channels - email, live chat, and Slack - using your knowledge, tools, and policies.

The **Internal Agent** (also referred to as just "Parahelp") builds it. You prompt it in natural language. It builds your flows, tests them against your real tickets, and stages everything for your approval.

## How you work with Parahelp

You do all of this in [tasks](/platform/tasks), following one loop:

1. **Build.** Describe the flow you want in natural language - the policies, knowledge, and tool connections it combines - and the Internal Agent builds it. See [Build](/internal-agent/build).
2. **Test.** Simulations replay your historical tickets against the draft change, and production tests verify your tool connections. See [Test](/internal-agent/test).
3. **Release.** Changes ship as releases - reviewed, versioned, revertable - and you set your agent live on your channels: email, live chat, and Slack. Nothing goes live without your approval. See [Release](/internal-agent/release).
4. **Improve.** Automations watch your tools and your queue. The Internal Agent reports the impact of every release and proposes the next build. See [Improve](/internal-agent/improve).

The loop continuously improves your Customer Agent. It learns from the tickets it handles, from what your team ships across your connected tools, and from every release you publish.
