Parahelp is an AI support solution built for fast-moving software companies. It integrates with your existing ticketing system and functions as a team member to resolve tickets end-to-end.

The platform consists of two agents:

The **Customer Agent** is customer-facing, with scoped tool connections and context to safely resolve customer issues, either autonomously or with human approval. See [Customer Agent](/customer-agent/introduction).

The **Internal Agent** (also referred to as just "Parahelp") is internal-facing, with broader connections and the ability to change the Customer Agent's configurations, incl. building secure tool connections. See [Internal Agent](/internal-agent/introduction).

## How you work with Parahelp

You do all of this in [tasks](/platform/tasks). It starts with an onboarding task, where the Internal Agent analyzes your last 500-1,000 resolved tickets along with your existing context to build the first version of your Customer Agent. From there, the two agents run a continuous improvement loop:

1. **Build** _(Internal Agent)._ You describe the support flow you want in natural language, and the Internal Agent builds the scoped policies, knowledge, and tool connections it needs. See [Build](/internal-agent/build).
2. **Test** _(Internal Agent)._ Before any configuration change ships, the Internal Agent tests it against your historical support tickets, and production tests verify your tool connections. See [Test](/internal-agent/test).
3. **Resolve** _(Customer Agent)._ The Customer Agent resolves tickets end-to-end across your channels - email, live chat, and Slack - acting only through the configurations you released. See [Customer Agent](/customer-agent/introduction).
4. **Identify gaps** _(Internal Agent)._ When the Customer Agent lacks context, it triggers the Internal Agent to investigate using its broader context and return with proposed changes, starting a new loop. Automations watch your tools and your queue, and the Internal Agent reports the impact of every release. See [Identify gaps](/internal-agent/identify-gaps).

Releases are your gate on that loop. The agents run the loop, but nothing reaches a customer until your team approves it: every change ships as a release you can review, version, and revert. See [Release](/internal-agent/release).

The loop continuously improves your Customer Agent. It learns from the tickets it handles, from what your team ships across your connected tools, and from every release you publish.
