## The onboarding task

When you finish the onboarding flow, the Internal Agent runs the onboarding task. It connects to your ticketing system, analyzes historical tickets, and researches everything you've connected to set up baseline Customer Agent configurations: Memory, knowledge, and standard tools.

It then creates a set of setup todos. They appear on the [Tasks page](/platform/chats) and in your [Inbox](/platform/inbox), and take you from a baseline Customer Agent to resolving tickets and building complex configurations.

## Complete the setup todos

Start each todo with one click. The exact todos depend on your setup, but they typically cover:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up triaging rules">
    Intelligently triage specific ticket categories or customers to specific teams when the Customer Agent hands a ticket off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test against real scenarios">
    Run test simulations based on historical tickets, and review the replies, reasoning, and tool calls. See [Testing](/customer-agent/testing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Set up the workflow in your ticketing system that assigns tickets to your Customer Agent. See [Ticketing System](/ticketing-systems).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up initial automations">
    Turn on the first automations, like a daily performance report, knowledge gap detection from handled tickets, and keeping synced help center pages fresh. See [Automations](/platform/continuous-tasks).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the setup todos

Your Customer Agent is live and resolving tickets.

From here, it keeps getting better: the Internal Agent creates new todos as it finds knowledge gaps, tool ideas, and improvement opportunities.
