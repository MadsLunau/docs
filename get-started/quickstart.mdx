The onboarding task has built your Customer Agent and standard ticketing tools. From here, this page takes you from your first resolved ticket to a support setup that keeps getting better.

## The onboarding task

When you finish the onboarding flow, the Internal Agent runs the onboarding task. It connects to your ticketing system, analyzes your historical tickets, and researches everything you've connected to draft your Customer Agent's baseline configurations: its memory, knowledge, and standard ticketing tools.

It then creates a set of setup todos, each with the prompt and context already written. They appear on the [Tasks page](/platform/chats) and in your [Inbox](/platform/inbox), and take you from a built Customer Agent to tickets resolved in production.

## Complete the setup todos

Start each todo with one click, review the work, and approve. The exact todos depend on your setup, but they typically cover:

- **Set up your main ticket categories:** configure the memory, knowledge, and tools for your most common ticket types, based on your historical tickets.
- **Define what is out of scope:** which ticket types transfer to your team, and where they go.
- **Test against real scenarios:** run test simulations based on historical tickets, and review the replies, reasoning, and tool calls before anything goes live. See [Testing](/customer-agent/testing).
- **Go live:** set up the workflow in your ticketing system that assigns tickets to your Customer Agent. See [Ticketing System](/ticketing-systems).
- **Set up your first automation:** a recurring task that reports performance and finds knowledge gaps as tickets come in. See [Automations](/platform/continuous-tasks).

## After the setup todos

Your Customer Agent is live and resolving tickets. From here, it keeps getting better: the Internal Agent creates new todos as it finds knowledge gaps, tool ideas, and improvement opportunities, and [automations](/platform/continuous-tasks) keep its knowledge fresh as your product changes.

Questions along the way? Ask the Internal Agent, or check the [FAQ](/resources/faq).
