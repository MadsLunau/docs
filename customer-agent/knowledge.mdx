title: "Knowledge"
description: "What your Customer Agent can search."
---

There are two types of knowledge:

- **Knowledge snippets:** Internal knowledge the Customer Agent can search, like an upcoming launch or a policy nuance that isn't public.
- **Web pages:** Your scraped web pages, available for the Customer Agent to search. They're continuously synced through an automation set up as part of the onboarding flow. Crawling respects your site's robots.txt, so if your site blocks crawlers the sync can complete with no pages. If that happens, reach out and we'll allowlist your approved domain to bypass robots.txt and resync.

See your live knowledge and its version history - who published what and when, with a diff for every change - on the [Configurations page in the app](https://app.parahelp.com/configurations).
