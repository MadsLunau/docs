---
title: "Inbox"
description: "Your todos and ongoing tasks in one place."
---

In the Inbox, you can see your:

- **Todos:** work the Internal Agent created and assigned to you or your team, ready to start with one click.
- **Ongoing tasks**, in one of three stages:
  - **In progress:** the Internal Agent is working.
  - **In review:** the task has pending actions waiting for your approval. See [Release](/internal-agent/release).
  - **Done:** no pending actions.

Todos also appear on the Tasks page. See [Tasks](/platform/tasks).

You can also route notifications to Slack. See [Settings → Notifications](/platform/settings#notifications).
