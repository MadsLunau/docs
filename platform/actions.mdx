---
title: "Releases"
description: "The release page is a changelog that holds every update you or the team has made to your Customer Agent. You can use it to stay in sync on updates made across the team."
---

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-81.png "Image")
</Frame>

When you approve a configuration Action in a task, it becomes a release on the "Releases" page. This could be updating a memory file, building a tool, or adding a new web page. Open a release to see exactly what changed, line by line.
