## 2. Choose the teammate

Parahelp replies as a Front teammate. You can use any teammate you want, so there is no need to create a dedicated Parahelp account.

When Parahelp validates your token, it looks up your teammates so you can confirm which one acts as the support agent.

## 3. Enter the token in Parahelp

Give Parahelp the API token when it asks. Parahelp validates it and identifies your Front company automatically.

Parahelp will then ask you to confirm which Front teammate acts as the support agent.

Optionally, you can also pick a specific outbound channel for replies.

## 5. Enter the app UID and secret in Parahelp

Submit the app UID and app secret together in the form.

The connection is live once the token is validated, the teammate is confirmed, and the app UID and secret are submitted.
