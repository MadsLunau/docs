<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the developer app in Intercom">
    Go to **Settings → Developer Hub**, click **New app**, and name it **Parahelp**.

    <Frame>
      ![Image](/images/image-76.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      ![Image](/images/image-77.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      ![Image](/images/image-78.png)
    </Frame>

    Open the app you just created.

    {/* SCREENSHOT: Intercom Developer Hub, New app */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set permissions">
    Under **Authentication**, enable the following permissions. Intercom's webhook topics are permission-gated, so the app needs all of them before the webhook step:

    - Read conversations
    - Write conversations
    - Read tickets
    - Write tickets
    - Read admins
    - Update admins
    - Read and list users and companies
    - Read and write users
    - Write users and companies
    - Read tags
    - Write tags
    - Write data attributes
    - Export message data
    - Read and write custom object instances
    - Read status of jobs

    <Frame>
      ![Image](/images/image-20.png)
    </Frame>

    Then click **Save**.

    {/* SCREENSHOT: Authentication permissions */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the support agent teammate">
    Create or choose a real dedicated Intercom teammate account for the agent, with its own email address.

    We recommend `parahelp@yourcompany.com` or `<agent-name>@yourcompany.com`: a real user with a real mailbox, invited like any teammate, not an email alias. Do not use a bot or machine user.

    Invite it to Intercom and grant it the role it needs for support work. After the token is saved, Parahelp looks this teammate up by email so you can confirm it as the support agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your access token in Parahelp">
    In the Intercom app, open **Configure → Authentication** and copy the **Access token**. See [Intercom's token guide](https://developers.intercom.com/docs/build-an-integration/learn-more/authentication) for reference.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 06 07 At 17 12 50](/images/Screenshot_2026-06-07_at_17.12.50.png)
    </Frame>

    Paste it into the secure form when Parahelp asks. Parahelp validates it, automatically detects your workspace and region, and looks up your teammates so you can confirm which one acts as the support agent. No replies are sent until you confirm it.

    {/* SCREENSHOT: Parahelp secure form */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure webhooks">
    In the app settings, open **Webhooks** and set the Endpoint URL to `https://webhooks.parahelp.com/v3/intercom/events`.

    Then subscribe to both the conversation and ticket topic families, so either format works in your workspace:

    - conversation.user.created
    - conversation.user.replied
    - conversation.admin.replied
    - conversation.admin.noted
    - conversation.admin.assigned
    - conversation.admin.closed
    - conversation.admin.opened
    - conversation.admin.snoozed
    - conversation.admin.unsnoozed
    - ticket.created
    - ticket.contact.replied
    - ticket.admin.replied
    - ticket.note.created
    - ticket.admin.assigned
    - ticket.state.updated
    - ticket.closed

    Tag events are not needed.

    {/* SCREENSHOT: Webhooks configuration */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the client secret in Parahelp">
    Open **Basic information** and copy the **Client secret**.

    <Frame>
      ![Image](/images/image-25.png)
    </Frame>

    Paste it into the secure form when Parahelp asks. Parahelp confirms the connection once the token, support agent, and client secret are all in place.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once connected, [assign conversations to Parahelp](/guides/intercom-assigning).
