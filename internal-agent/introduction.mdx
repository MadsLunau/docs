The Internal Agent configures, tests, releases, and identifies gaps for your Customer Agent. Each step happens in a [task](/platform/tasks).

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Configure" icon="sliders" href="/internal-agent/configure">
    Describe what you want, and the Internal Agent configures it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Test" icon="flask" href="/internal-agent/test">
    Simulate on your historical tickets before anything ships.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Release" icon="rocket" href="/internal-agent/release">
    Approve changes to publish them, versioned and revertable.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Identify gaps" icon="chart-line" href="/internal-agent/identify-gaps">
    Prompt the Internal Agent for a report, or let automations surface gaps as your product changes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For the full loop, start with [Configure](/internal-agent/configure).
