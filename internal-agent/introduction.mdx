The Internal Agent identifies the gaps that start each loop, then builds and tests the changes to your Customer Agent's configurations and stages them for your approval. Each step happens in a [task](/platform/tasks).

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Identify gaps" icon="chart-line" href="/internal-agent/identify-gaps">
    Prompt the Internal Agent for a report, or let automations surface gaps as your product changes.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Build" icon="sliders" href="/internal-agent/build">
    Describe the support flow you want, and the Internal Agent builds it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Test" icon="flask" href="/internal-agent/test">
    Simulate on your historical tickets before anything ships.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Release" icon="rocket" href="/internal-agent/release">
    Your gate on the loop - approve changes to publish them, versioned and revertable.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
