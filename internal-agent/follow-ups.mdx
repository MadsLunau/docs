You can have the Customer Agent write personalized follow ups with context you've provided i. 

Companies use follow-up to close the loop once you've fixed a bug or shipped a new feature, keep customer's up to date during an incident, or check in on users who haven't gotten back in a ticket. 

The Internal Agent doesn't write each customer's message itself. It hands every ticket to your Customer Agent, which reads that ticket's own history and writes a reply tailored to it, following your instruction. Depending on the ticket, it can reply, resolve, or transfer.

## Proactive follow-ups

Prompt the Internal Agent in a [task](/platform/tasks) to follow up on a group of tickets. You might have fixed a bug and now want to follow up with everyone who reported it in the last 6 months. 

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prompt the follow-up">
    Tell the Internal Agent what information you want send and to who. Based on your general description, it will research and identify the relevant tickets to follow up on and what to instructions to give to the Customer Agent.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the action">
    The follow-up is staged as a single action in the "Actions" tab, so you can review it before anything goes out. See [Release](/internal-agent/release).
  </Step>

  <Step title="It sends">
    Once approved, your Customer Agent works each ticket and sends its reply. Messages are spread out over time so you don't contact everyone at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Automatic follow-ups

It is common to have [Automations](/platform/automations)running identifying relevant tickets to follow up on.

When your Customer Agent replied last and the customer has gone quiet, it can follow up on its own:

- **First check:** it sends a friendly check-in.
- **Next check:** if the customer still hasn't replied, it closes the ticket, optionally with a short closing message.

It won't add new information or keep chasing beyond that, and a customer reply stops the sequence.

<Note>
  Automatic follow-ups send on a schedule with no approval step, so this is a managed feature. Contact the Parahelp team to turn it on.
</Note>

## Configure your follow-up messages

Automatic follow-ups pull their wording from your **Follow-up memory**, which you edit by prompting the Internal Agent like any other configuration. It holds:

- a **check-in message** for the first follow-up,
- a **closing message** for when the ticket is closed,
- optional personalization, such as the customer's first name,
- your timing rules for when follow-ups should go out.

Changes are staged as an action and published as a release, so you review the exact wording before it goes live. See [Memory](/customer-agent/memory) and [Release](/internal-agent/release).

## Follow up on a schedule

To follow up on pending tickets every day without prompting each time, set up a **Daily follow-up** [automation](/platform/automations). It looks at tickets assigned to your Customer Agent that have been waiting on the customer, and drafts personalized follow-ups: a first check-in if none has gone out yet, or a second check-in and a close if one already has. It notifies you in Slack whenever it drafts something, and the drafts follow the same approval rules as any personalized follow-up.

## Approvals and control

- **Personalized follow-ups** need admin approval by default. An admin can enable auto-approve for a specific task or automation so they send without waiting. See [Settings → Members](/platform/settings#members).
- **Automatic follow-ups** send directly with no approval, which is why they're opt-in and managed by Parahelp.
- Follow-ups stop as soon as the customer replies, and a pending follow-up action can be cancelled before it runs.
